OUR MISSION

Founded in 2018 on Merton Street, our journey began when neighbours decided to take a stand against litter in local parks. What started as a small effort quickly grew, and by late 2019, we expanded our reach to neighbourhoods across Ontario. Our mission is to restore the lost beauty of our natural spaces and cultivate a greener future for every community.

We believe in protecting what we love most the vibrant nature and diverse neighbourhoods that make this province home. By preserving our local parks, we aim to make a lasting impact that goes beyond a simple cleanup. We strive to lead by example, providing the motivation for others to join our movement. Together, we can take direct action to safeguard our environment, ensuring Ontario’s natural splendour remains a source of pride for generations to come.

CREATE AN IMPACT

Every piece of litter removed from our sidewalks and parks creates a visible ripple effect right in our own backyard. Studies show that clean, well-maintained green spaces boost neighbourhood pride, lower local crime rates, and directly support surrounding property values—turning neglected corners into safe, welcoming gathering spots for families.

At the same time, keeping our streets tidy is our first line of defence for our shared environment: because roughly 80% of local waterway pollution starts on land, grassroots neighbourhood cleanups intercept tens of thousands of pounds of waste before it can wash down storm drains into our lakes and ravines.

When neighbours come together for just a few hours at a local park or sorting hub, we aren't just picking up trash—we are strengthening the bond between residents and proving that collective care can turn our streets into communities.
